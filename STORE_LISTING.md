# Store Listing Copy

Draft copy for the App Store and Google Play listings, built around the "social
layer over a shared library" pitch — lead with community/library/Rec, reading
is a utility feature mentioned near the end, not the headline. Character counts
are approximate; re-check against the current store limits at submission time.

**Google Play title (30 char max):** `MangaRecs: Manga Community`

## Subtitle / short description

**App Store subtitle (30 char max):** `Track, Rec & Talk Manga`
**Google Play short description (80 char max):** `Track your manga & manhwa library, Rec titles to friends, talk chapters.`

## Promotional text (App Store only, 170 char, editable anytime without review)

> The manga & manhwa community, finally in one place. Track your library, Rec
> titles to friends, and talk chapters without spoilers ruining it for anyone.

## Full description

```
MangaRecs is the social home for your manga & manhwa library.

Every series you touch lands on its own shelf — reading, completed,
bookmarked, downloaded — synced to your account so it's there no matter what
phone you're on. But it's not just your shelf. It's a place to actually be
part of the community around what you're reading, instead of tracking it
alone in a spreadsheet and talking about it in a dozen scattered group chats.

TRACK YOUR LIBRARY
Add manga, manhwa, manhua, webtoons, and novels the moment you find them.
Custom sorting, per-series progress, offline downloads.

FRIENDS & RECS
See what your friends are actually reading right now — not just what they
finished months ago. Send a "Rec" straight into their library, or just tell
them the chapter that wrecked you.

TALK WITHOUT SPOILERS
Every series has its own discussion threads, tagged by chapter, so someone
three chapters behind can still scroll through without getting burned.

BADGES, STREAKS & BRAGGING RIGHTS
250 badges to chase, Bronze-to-Mythic medal tiers, reading streaks, and a
Relic Vault of hand-drawn rewards — a real profile worth showing off, not one
trophy icon that changes color.

DISCOVER SOMETHING NEW
A swipeable feed tuned to your taste, mood-based picks, and an AI-flavored
"For You" tab that keeps adjusting instead of asking you to fill out a genre
survey once and forget it.

READ RIGHT IN THE APP
However you found a title — your library, a friend's Rec, or the feed — read
it without leaving MangaRecs. Scroll it like a webtoon or flip it like paged
manga, your call per series.

Free to use. No account required to browse.
```

## Notes for whoever fills out the actual listing form

- Don't name specific third-party reading sites anywhere in store copy, screenshots, or keywords — keep the reading feature described generically ("read right in the app," not by site name). This is independent of what the in-app site list contains.
- "AI-powered recommendations" in the description should stay accurate to whatever the For You tab is actually doing at submission time — don't overclaim ML sophistication that isn't there.
- Age rating questionnaire: answer based on actual in-app content (user-generated discussions, direct messages) — factor in the moderation/reporting tooling already built (block, report, moderation dashboard) when answering the UGC questions.

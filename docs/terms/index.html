What MangaRecs is

MangaRecs is a manga, manhwa, manhua, and webcomic discovery and progress-tracking app, with this website as its companion catalog and account hub. The app is currently in testing ahead of its App Store / Google Play release.

The app and site help you discover titles and keep track of what you're reading elsewhere — they don't replace or substitute the original publishers or platforms. Catalog metadata shown here (titles, cover art, synopses) is sourced from AniList's public API. MangaRecs is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by AniList, MyAnimeList, or any publisher, platform, or creator referenced in our catalog. See our About page for more, including how to report a catalog listing.

The one exception is our Creator Dashboard: verified creators may upload and publish their own original manga or webtoon series directly to MangaRecs. That content is hosted with the creator's permission as its rights holder — it's original work, not third-party licensed manga. By uploading, a creator confirms they own the rights to what they publish; content uploaded without the rights to do so may be removed.

FAQ

Questions, Answered

Is MangaRecs an app or a website?

MangaRecs is a mobile app for iOS and Android. This website is its companion — browse the catalog, and once you sign in here, your library stays in sync with the app.

Is it free?

Yes. Browsing the catalog here never requires a login, and the app itself is free — no paywall to track your library, talk to friends, or read.

Does MangaRecs host the actual chapters?

No. MangaRecs helps you discover titles and keep track of what you're reading — it doesn't host or distribute manga, manhwa, manhua, or webcomic content itself.

What formats does it support?

Manga, manhwa, manhua, webcomics, and novels — five formats, all in one catalog and one library.

When does it launch, and where?

MangaRecs is in final testing now, heading to the App Store and Google Play. No sideloaded APK — it'll be a normal store install on both platforms.

Do I need an account?

Not to browse. Sign in only when you want your saved titles and reading progress to sync between this site and the app.
